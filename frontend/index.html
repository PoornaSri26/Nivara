🚨 Immediate Crisis Resources

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, experiencing thoughts of self-harm, or undergoing a mental health crisis, please reach out to professional human support immediately:

United States & Canada:

Free, confidential, available 24/7 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
United Kingdom:
Call 116 123 (Samaritans) or 111
Text SHOUT to 85258
India:
Call 14416 (Tele-MANAS) or +91 9999 666 555
International:
Visit befrienders.org or contact your local emergency hospital.
